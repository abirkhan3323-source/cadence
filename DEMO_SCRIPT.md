### 0:50–1:20 — THE COACH RESPONDS (30 seconds)

**[Show the Feedback screen with the coaching response.]**

> "Cadence responds:"

**[Read the coaching, or let it display:]**

> *"This is the most common beginner hurdle — the descending thumb-under crossover. Isolate just the three-note crossover where you hesitate. Play those three notes ten times slowly, then add one note before it, then two. The instrument is the gym — you're building a neural pathway, not just repeating a scale."*

> "That's not 'practice more.' That's not generic encouragement. That's the 80/20 method — isolate the ONE thing that matters most. The exact same feedback a $60/hour teacher would give. But Maria got it on a Monday, two minutes after she practiced."
