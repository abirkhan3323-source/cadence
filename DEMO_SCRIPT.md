**What to show:** Use voice + piano audio together. This is THE key demo moment.

**Voiceover:**

> "Meet Maria. Twelve years old. Three weeks into piano. Her teacher sees her Thursdays. Today is Monday. She practiced her C major scale."

**[Click the "Play Piano" audio record button — it turns red, level meter animates]**

**Action:** Play a C major scale on a piano (or piano app, or a YouTube C major scale video through your speakers — needs to be actual piano sound, not voice).

**[Speak as Maria while the piano audio was just recorded — or play the voice as a recording through your speakers:]**

*Maria's voice (after the piano recording stops):*

**[Click Send to Coach. Loading animation plays — neural bars, 3-step indicator.]**

**[Feedback screen appears. READ the coaching response out loud:]**

**Show the coaching box with gold border. Point to it.**

> "Cadence detected EIGHT notes: C4, D4, E4, F4, G4, A4, B4, C5. At 120 BPM. One hesitation at the descending crossover. And here's the feedback:"

> *"The classic descending thumb-under crossover — the number one beginner hurdle. Isolate just the three-note crossover: finger three to thumb, at the exact spot where you hesitate. Play those three notes ten times slowly. The instrument is the gym — you're building a neural pathway, not just repeating a scale."*

> "That's not 'practice more.' That's specific. Cadence heard her play, identified the exact notes, spotted the hesitation, and gave ONE thing to fix. The 80/20 method. The same feedback a sixty-dollar-an-hour teacher would give. But Maria got it on a Monday, two minutes after she practiced."
