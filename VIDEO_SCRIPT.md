## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Voice Setup
- **Narrator:** Warm, clear, professional tone. Think documentary-style. ElevenLabs "Adam" or "Antoni" voice.
- **Maria:** Younger, natural voice. ElevenLabs "Bella" or similar. Keep it casual — she's talking to a coach, not performing.

### Screen Recording Settings
- **Resolution:** 1920×1080, 30fps
- **Browser:** Chrome, bookmarks bar hidden, dark mode
- **Zoom:** 100% (do NOT zoom in — it cuts off the floating particles)
- **Cursor:** Show cursor, add click highlights if your editor supports it

### Editing Tips
- Fade transitions between clips (0.3s crossfade)
- Voiceover should lead the visuals by ~0.5s — say it, then show it
- Add subtle background piano music at 10-15% volume (Chopin Nocturne or similar — royalty-free)
- URL stays on screen for the final 10 seconds
- Total runtime: exactly 2:00. Trim aggressively.
